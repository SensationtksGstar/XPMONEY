YNAB (You Need A Budget) is one of the best-known budgeting apps. If you landed here, you have probably tried it or considered paying for it. This article compares it with other options that make sense in Portugal.

Two rules we follow: **we only state what we can confirm in a source** (linked at each point), and **we don't quote third-party prices** we can't confirm. App store prices change by country, by plan and over time. This article uses data checked on **20 October 2026**.

> We are XP-Money, so we have an obvious interest. We put ourselves on the list with our limitations in plain sight, and we say when you should **not** use us.

## YNAB

- **Price:** $14.99 per month or $109 per year, in US dollars, with a 34-day free trial and no card required when you sign up directly on the website. Source: [YNAB pricing page](https://www.ynab.com/pricing).
- **Bank connections:** according to [YNAB's support page](https://support.ynab.com/en_us/direct-import-in-the-uk-and-eu-an-overview-Syae1z_A9), direct import in Europe is available for select banks. Check in the app whether your bank is included before relying on it.
- **The method:** zero-based budgeting, where every euro gets a job before it is spent. It works well for people who enjoy applying it, but it takes effort, and that is the main factor in the decision.
- **Before you subscribe:** check the interface and support language, and whether a dollar price works for you.

**Makes sense if:** you like zero-based budgeting and are happy to pay in dollars for a rigorous method.

## Wallet (BudgetBakers)

- The [Portuguese App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/pt/app/wallet-finan%C3%A7as-pessoais/id1032467659) lists Portuguese among the supported languages. Check whether the translation is European or Brazilian Portuguese.
- According to [Santander](https://www.santander.pt/salto/apps-para-controlar-despesas-portugal), there is a free manual-entry version, while bank connection and automatic sync are part of the Premium plan. The connection works in read-only mode, varies by bank and account type, and may ask you to re-authenticate.
- The App Store listing shows several paid plans at different prices. Check the price of the plan you want in the store on your own device.

**Makes sense if:** you want to link bank accounts and are willing to pay for it, once you have confirmed your bank is supported.

## Boonzi

- According to [Santander](https://www.santander.pt/salto/apps-para-controlar-despesas-portugal), it was developed in Portugal for Portuguese households. After a 30-day trial you can keep using the free version, which has fewer features and shows ads. Paid licences exist.
- The mobile app works mostly as a companion to the desktop version, and some functions need a paid licence.

**Makes sense if:** you prefer working on a computer, want software built in Portugal, and don't mind a more limited mobile version.

## Moneyboard

- The [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/budget-expense-moneyboard/id625887028) says it is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, with Portuguese among its languages, and Pro plans (monthly, yearly or lifetime). Prices in the Portuguese store may differ from the US store, so check them on your device.

**Makes sense if:** you are in the Apple ecosystem and want a budgeting app that syncs via iCloud.

## Revolut Analytics

- [Revolut's help centre](https://help.revolut.com/help/app-features/budgeting-and-analytics/how-does-the-analytics-feature-work/) explains that Analytics brings your spending and scheduled payments from all your Revolut accounts into one place. You can create your own categories and change a transaction's category.
- By design it only sees what goes through Revolut: if your salary and fixed bills are at another bank, the picture is incomplete.

**Makes sense if:** Revolut is your main account and you only want to see where the money goes, with no budget or goals.

## Excel or Google Sheets

- It costs nothing, gives you full control and maximum privacy.
- The cost is discipline: you have to log everything and build the categorisation, summaries and alerts yourself. We have no data on how many people give up, so we won't make up a percentage. If you have abandoned a spreadsheet before, you know what this means.

**Makes sense if:** you are methodical and enjoy building your own system.

## XP-Money

- **Price:** a free plan with no time limit. Premium is €4.99 per month or €39.99 per year.
- **On the free plan:** 50/30/20 budget, transaction logging, 2 active goals, 1 active debt in Debt Killer (avalanche or snowball method) and 3 introductory courses.
- **On Premium:** receipt scanning by photo and PDF/CSV statement import (both read by AI, so always check the values before saving), unlimited goals and debts, and the advanced courses.
- **Interface in European Portuguese** (and English), with a mascot that evolves as you log transactions and improve your financial score.
- **You import your bank statements without giving us your password.** We don't connect directly to your bank.

**Limitations:**
- **No automatic bank connection.** You import statements or log by hand. It was a deliberate privacy choice, but it costs convenience.
- **It is a PWA, not a native app.** You install it to your home screen from the browser, but it isn't in the app stores.

**Don't use us if:** you need automatic bank connection or a native app from the stores.

## How to choose

- You want zero-based budgeting and accept the price: **YNAB**, after the 34-day trial.
- You want to link bank accounts: **Wallet**, after confirming your bank is supported.
- You prefer Portuguese-made software for the computer: **Boonzi**.
- Revolut is your main account and you only want to see spending: **Revolut Analytics**.
- You are methodical and like building your own system: **Excel**.
- You want an app in European Portuguese that helps you build the habit, without handing over bank credentials: **XP-Money**.
